What Answer check protects

When you mark an assignment as graded, Answer check keeps the answer to each problem out of anything the app writes for you, so Ask, guides and flashcards can explain the ideas and write similar problems without handing you the solution. It works only on problems with a worked key, it cannot catch an answer written in an unusual form, and it does not check your own work or stop you from looking the answer up elsewhere.

Flashcard keyboard shortcuts

When on, on the flashcards page Space shows the answer and the keys 1 to 4 grade the card. They never work while you type in a field.
